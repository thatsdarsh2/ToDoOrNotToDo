02 / PLAN YOUR TASKS

Keep the details close.

Add a title and optional note, then choose a due date, priority, category, repeat schedule, or reminder. Select a task card to focus on it, complete it, or review its details.

03 / SEE THE WEEK

Find a rhythm that works.

The overview counts open, due-today, and high-priority tasks. The ring tracks what you finish today; completing a task also raises the calm waterline. Open Week agenda to see dated tasks by day and move between weeks.
